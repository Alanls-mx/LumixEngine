# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Local businesses, service providers, clinics, agencies, and commerce in Brazil that rely on WhatsApp to capture leads and convert customers, but lose sales due to delayed response times and lack of automated scheduling.

## Product Purpose
Deliver high-speed WhatsApp automation and high-conversion landing pages that respond to customer inquiries in under 2 seconds, qualify leads 24/7, automate appointment bookings, and organize customer pipelines without technical complexity.

## Positioning
Practical, high-speed agency for local commerce delivering direct ROI ("response in 2s, 24/7 sales") and streamlined WhatsApp & CRM setups without tech friction or bureaucratic overhead.

## Operating Context
- Brazilian local business market relying heavily on WhatsApp (wa.me direct links) for sales discussions and appointment bookings.
- Local mobile-first visitors seeking instant quotes, business hours, and fast answers.
- Simple CRM and lead pipeline tracking for business owners to follow up on hot leads.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- **Speed Guarantee**: Responds to customer inquiries in ~2 seconds.
- **WhatsApp Integration**: Automated routing, interactive menus, FAQ handling, lead qualification, and handoff to human support.
- **Landing Pages**: Fast-loading, responsive, mobile-first pages optimized for local SEO, Google Ads, and direct WhatsApp conversion.
- **Lead & CRM Management**: Centralized contact management and simplified lead stages to prevent lost deals.
- **Language**: Portuguese (pt-BR).

## Brand Commitments
- **Name**: LumixEngine
- **Tagline**: Automações & Sites para Negócios Locais
- **Voice & Tone**: Direct, high-energy, confident, pragmatic, and conversion-focused.
- **Key Promises**: "Atendimento Instantâneo", "Sem Burocracia", "Suporte Local", "24h captação ativa".

## Evidence on Hand
- Incumbent landing page implementation in React 18 + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind CSS + Framer Motion.
- Core content, services structure, mock WhatsApp chat interactive component, FAQ, and testimonials configured in `src/constants/content.ts` and `src/components/`.

## Product Principles
1. **Speed is Conversion**: Every second of delay in WhatsApp loses customers; speed (< 2s) is the primary competitive edge.
2. **Zero Tech Friction**: Solutions must be clear, turnkey, and immediately usable by local business owners without technical complexity.
3. **Action-Oriented Copy**: Clear, unambiguous calls to action driving direct WhatsApp conversations and qualified bookings.
4. **Mobile-First Reality**: The majority of local customers interact from smartphones; mobile responsiveness and fast loading are paramount.
